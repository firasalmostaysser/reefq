# Marketing plan: Facebook and Instagram first

Goal: a steady flow of couples who order **without a conversation**. Every link we publish is tagged, so the Studio shows which post or ad brought each order (Orders → Review → **Source**).

## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

> **L'invitation qu'on ouvre comme une vraie enveloppe : chaque famille reçoit la sienne, à son nom, et vous voyez qui vient en direct.**

Why couples pay: it looks premium (envelope, wax seal, three languages), it ends the "vous êtes combien ?" calls (personal links with reserved seats), it costs less than printed cards, and they can prepare it and see it **before paying**.

Who: Tunisian couples (and their sisters, mothers and cousins who organise) 3 to 9 months before the wedding, fiançailles or contrat. Facebook reaches the families; Instagram reaches the couple.

## 2. Setup (do once, before the first post)

- **Facebook Page** "Reefq · Invitations digitales" + **Instagram** professional account, linked in Meta Business Suite. Profile picture: the logo on ivory. Cover: the envelope with the teal seal.
- **Bio / About:** `Invitations de mariage qui s'ouvrent comme une vraie enveloppe 💌 Un lien à son nom pour chaque famille · Réponses en direct · FR · عربي · EN · Dès 149 DT` and the link `https://reefq.netlify.app/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio` (Facebook: `utm_source=facebook`).
- **WhatsApp Business app** on the Reefq number: profile, catalogue (3 offers), greeting message, away message, and the quick replies from `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 5.
- **Automatic replies** (Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations): keyword **LIEN** / **رابط** / **prix** → reply with the demo link and the order link. Frequently asked questions → the quick-reply texts. This answers comments-to-DM and DMs at night without you.
- **Pinned post** and **Highlights** on Instagram: "Démo", "Offres", "Comment ça marche", "Avis".

## 3. Tagged links (always use them)

```
https://reefq.netlify.app/?utm_source=<where>&utm_medium=<format>&utm_campaign=<what>
```

| Field | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `utm_source` | `facebook`, `instagram`, `whatsapp`, `tiktok`, `partner` |
| `utm_medium` | `post`, `reel`, `story`, `ad`, `bio`, `dm`, `group` |
| `utm_campaign` | the content id, e.g. `reel-ouverture`, `post-offres`, `ad-oct-1`, partner name |

Two links are automatic: every invitation footer ("Créée avec amour par Reefq") brings guests with `utm_source=invitation`, and the thank-you message after each wedding carries the couple's `?ref=RQ-XXXXX` link (-10 % for their friends, applied automatically).

Brand rules apply to every visual (see `docs/DESIGNER.md` section 1). Videos have no audio track; put the message in on-screen text.

## 5. Cadence

- **3 reels** (Mon, Wed, Fri, 20:00–21:30), **4 posts** (Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun), **stories daily** (polls, "behind the scenes" of a template, a reply counter).
- Post reels on Instagram and Facebook (cross-post) and in **WhatsApp status**.
- Answer every comment within the day (auto-replies cover the night).

## 7. Paid ads (start in week 3)

Organic first: two weeks of posts show which reel people watch and share. Then:

- **Budget:** 10–15 DT/day, one campaign "Ventes" or "Trafic" to the landing page with the tagged link (`utm_medium=ad&utm_campaign=ad-oct-1`).
- **Creative:** the two best reels from weeks 1–2, as they are.
- **Audience:** Tunisia, 21–38, interests wedding / engagement / wedding planning (and "Engaged" life events if offered), French and Arabic.
- **Rule:** after 7 days, keep an ad only if it brings orders at under **60 DT per paid order** (look at Studio sources). Double the budget of the winner, stop the rest.
- Meta ads need a card that pays in foreign currency; check yours works before week 3.

## 8. Partners (word of mouth, almost free)

Salles des fêtes, traiteurs, pâtissiers, wedding planners, henna artists and print shops see couples months before the wedding.

- Offer each partner a personal link `?utm_source=partner&utm_campaign=<name>` and **20 DT per paid order** it brings (you see it in the order's Source; pay monthly by transfer).
- Leave a small A6 card with a QR code to the demo at their counter (Studio → Deliver can make QR codes; or use the demo link).
- Start with 5 partners near you this month.
